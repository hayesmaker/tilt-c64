    Gates

    Every gate is always there, as one of two kinds per level (5 = level 5 and up): ticked = fire gate (yellow: fire flips it open for a moment), unticked = tilt gate (cyan: a bar that only opens while the board tilts against it). Pick a gate, then click the board with the Gate tool to move it (R rotates). A maze can have 0-16 gates (the original mazes have 8).
